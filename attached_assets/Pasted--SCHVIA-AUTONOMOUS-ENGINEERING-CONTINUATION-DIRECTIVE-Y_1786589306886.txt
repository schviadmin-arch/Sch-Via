---

# 1. WHAT SCHVIA ACTUALLY IS

SchVIA is a technology company building a Digital Operating System for institutions.

Schools are the first market.

The first product must solve a real, narrow problem extremely well:

**making institutional information organized, accessible, connected and useful without forcing staff to repeatedly manage the same information.**

For the MVP, the primary focus is:

* Student records
* Classes
* Teachers
* Attendance
* Principal oversight
* Basic parent visibility where appropriate
* Institutional activity/history
* Reliable data
* Offline attendance
* Clear communication of what is happening

The customer is the institution.

The institution owns the workspace.

The institution's authorized people use the system.

SchVIA must eventually be capable of expanding beyond schools without rebuilding its fundamental philosophy.

# 2. THE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Never think:

"Here is an attendance feature."

Think:

"A teacher records an attendance event involving a student inside a school. What other parts of the institution should logically know about or react to that event?"

For example:

Teacher records:

Student A → Absent

The system should logically be capable of causing:

* attendance record update
* student attendance history update
* class attendance calculation update
* school attendance calculation update
* principal dashboard update
* activity/audit event
* notification eligibility
* parent visibility if enabled
* analytics update
* relevant timeline/history update

The user should enter information once.

The system should do the connected work.

This principle applies throughout the product.

# 6. THE SCHOOL OWNER / PRINCIPAL MODEL

Do NOT assume that one person performs every administrative task.

A school may have:

* Principal
* Headmaster
* Proprietor
* Administrator
* Academic coordinator
* Secretary
* Class teacher
* Subject teacher
* Other authorized staff

The person who creates the school becomes the initial owner/administrator according to the appropriate model.

That person should be able to establish the institution and delegate responsibilities.

The system must support controlled delegation.

Example:

Principal creates school.

Principal may create/invite:

Administrator

Academic coordinator

Teacher

Other permitted staff

The principal should NOT be required to personally enter every student, class, subject or operational record.

The product must reason about delegation.

If a task logically belongs to an administrator or teacher, the system should allow the appropriate role to perform it while maintaining oversight.
